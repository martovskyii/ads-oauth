Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the internal tooling ("ADS") operated by Daniil Bezsazhnyi for personal advertising-management work. The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent via Google OAuth, the tool accesses Google Ads, Google Analytics (read-only), Google Sheets and Google Drive data of the owner's own Google accounts, solely to build reports, spreadsheets and documents and to store encrypted backups.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed locally on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally and never transmitted anywhere except to Google's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
